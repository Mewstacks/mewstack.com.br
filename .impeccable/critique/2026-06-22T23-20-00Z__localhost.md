## Anti-Patterns Verdict
LLM: Low slop. Distinctive — real headset-cat mascot, official hot-pink/charcoal/light, PT-BR voice, data-chip motif, tagline marquee. No gradient text, no glass-default, no hero-metric template, no identical card grids. The one tell: a pink section-kicker label repeats above ~5 sections (sentence-case, not the banned uppercase-tracked form, but still a repeated cadence).
Deterministic scan: `detect.mjs --json src index.html` → `[]`, exit 0. Clean.
Overlay: skipped (avoid disrupting user dev server); fallback = clean CLI + screenshot review.

## Overall Impression
Confident, memorable, unmistakably MewStack. Biggest opportunity: the founder-card image (baked-in tiny contact text, illegible on mobile, duplicates the Contact section) and trimming the repeated section-kicker cadence.

## What's Working
- Hero: mascot pulling source-data chips literally shows "data processing" — concept matches positioning.
- Committed palette: charcoal Manifesto/Contact bands punctuate the light surfaces; pink stays high-energy without failing contrast (pink-deep on light, pink-bright on charcoal).
- Serviços as a divided list (not a card grid) and a real numbered 3-step Processo = honest use of sequence.

## Priority Issues
- [P2] Founder-card raster: small baked-in phone/email illegible on mobile, duplicates Contact. Fix: replace with a clean photo of Germano + native text, or a designed responsive card. Command: /impeccable layout
- [P2] Repeated pink section-kicker above ~5 sections leans toward AI section-grammar. Fix: vary cadence — drop some, or fold into the heading. Command: /impeccable typeset
- [P3] No skip-to-content link; keyboard users tab through nav every time. Fix: add visually-hidden skip link. Command: /impeccable harden
- [P3] Mobile loses in-page nav (Serviços/Processo/Estúdio hidden, only CTA). Fix: small menu or anchor row. Command: /impeccable adapt
